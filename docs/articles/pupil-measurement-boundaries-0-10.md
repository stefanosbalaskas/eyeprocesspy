# Pupil Measurement Boundaries in Multimodal IRT

> Python counterpart of the frozen `eyeprocess` 0.11.1 article. The R 0.11.1 source remains the scientific reference.

Pupil measurements require explicit handling of baseline, luminance, gaze position, quality, blink/interpolation burden, device/session structure and missingness. M3 therefore treats pupil as a measurement channel with nuisance adjustment rather than a direct label for an internal mental state.

`eyeprocesspy` preserves this boundary in simulation, audits, negative controls, functional-score bridging and documentation.
